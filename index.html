About

Health data, scattered everywhere

Lab results and checkups end up as paper printouts, PDFs, and photos spread across different clinics. MedSnapshot brings them together: upload a report or a photo, and the service automatically recognizes biomarkers, builds a single health history, and explains what a term or value means and which type of doctor handles it — plus reminders to take a medication or book a follow-up checkup. It never tells you what condition you have or how to treat it.

Platform

Built for clinical-grade data

Everything a care platform needs to ingest, secure, and ship structured health data.

AI biomarker extraction

Pulls structured biomarkers and clinical values out of lab reports, scanned PDFs, and free-text notes using computer vision and LLM pipelines.

Enterprise security

End-to-end encryption in transit and at rest, HIPAA-aligned data handling, and full audit logging on every access.

Built to scale

A versioned REST API on a Spring Boot backend, designed for high-throughput clinical integrations and predictable latency.
